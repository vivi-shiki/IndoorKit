These files are renamed copies of the 1K colour, OpenGL normal and roughness maps. The colour map and, at high quality, the normal map are embedded in the campus GLBs. All model geometry is original to this repository and follows its MIT licence.

`paving-color-512.jpg` and `paving-normal-512.jpg` are 512 × 512 resamples of the colour and normal maps (Lanczos, JPEG quality 82 and 88). The generator embeds these smaller copies to keep the campus downloads small.
